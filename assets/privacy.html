Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Welcome to LinkUp. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account info: Your email address, username, and display name when you register.
  • Profile photo: Stored as compressed base64 data directly in Firestore (no third-party storage).
  • Messages: Text messages you send are stored in Firestore and are only accessible to the conversation participants.
  • Usage data: Last seen timestamps to show activity status.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the LinkUp chat service.
  • To authenticate your identity securely via Firebase Auth.
  • To display your profile to other users.
  • To deliver real-time messages between users.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore in the asia-southeast1 region. We do not use Firebase Storage — profile images are stored as compressed JPEG data within Firestore documents.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Your messages are private and only visible to you and the people you are chatting with.

5. Data Security

We use Firestore Security Rules to ensure users can only access their own data and conversations they are part of. All communications are encrypted in transit.

6. Your Rights

  • You may delete your account at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  • Account deletion removes your profile data from Firestore.
  • You may export your data from Profile → Settings → Export Data.

7. Children's Privacy

LinkUp is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

For questions, contact us at support@linkup.app
